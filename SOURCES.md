## Player IDs and Brood War races

- [Liquipedia Korean player reference](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Portal:Players/Korean) and [full player list](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Players/Full_Players_List): English player-ID mapping and race cross-checks for roster members.
- [Blizzard's official ASL Season 3 player listing](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20720238/asl-season-3-round-of-24-has-begun): historical but authoritative ID/race identity corroboration, not evidence of current team membership.
- [KCM statistics](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/KCM/Statistics) and [2026 Daily Proleague statistics](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Daily_Proleague/Statistics/2026): race cross-checks for the notable-player list.
- [RoyaL](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/RoyaL), [Sea](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Sea), [Calm](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Calm), and [soma results](https://liquipedia.net/starcraft/Soma/Results): additional identity checks.
- The official ASL S22 bracket independently verifies Leta and the seven other quarterfinalists' IDs and races.

Only player IDs appear on the guide. Capitalization varies by broadcast; Jaedong also appears as JD, and SnOw as Snow. Flash is listed with his primary/current race, Terran; his historical Random appearances do not make the whole current directory a Random roster. Brood War's hero is Zerg.
