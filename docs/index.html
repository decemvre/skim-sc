Kkonibugging joins star university team Sobyungdae

Kkonibugging, a 4-tier Protoss player, officially joined the Sobyungdae star university team, which posted a welcome message on October 29. In his own post he said he was not pushed into joining, thanked the team's fans and promised to work even harder. He also said he would carry many memories from BGM.

BGM leader and members say farewell to the star university

The BGM leader posted a long farewell on October 29, looking back on the team's cup runs and saying now was a good time to let go. He plans to spend the coming months streaming on his own and reflecting on himself. Shuu said she had made good friends at the team and would cheer from afar.
