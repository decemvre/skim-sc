SOOP to fund a monthly high-tier tournament, announced by Sarang-e

On October 19, after his broadcast, Sarang-e announced that SOOP will sponsor a monthly tournament worth ₩1,500,000 (about $1,070). It is a one-day, 16-player bracket for the top applicants by tier, with single games in the early rounds and best-of-three from the semifinals. Prize split and format are still being discussed.
