Brood War takes the Jamsil ice rink stage on the 17th

OSEN looks at StarCraft's return to the centre of Korean esports after the closures of pro league teams, a period when the game was often dismissed as a relic. The piece presents the coming event on a Jamsil ice rink as proof that the scene can last.

2026-10-16

SOOP and ASL: ten years together, Season 22 final at Lotte World on 17 Oct

StarCraft remains active as an esports game, with tournaments running for nearly ten years since the pro league and major teams disappeared. Online, hundreds of millions of people have watched matches. SOOP will host the Google Play ASL Season 22 final at the Jamsil Lotte World ice rink on 17 October, with a crowd of over 1,000 expected.

Source ↗

2026-10-16

Rush and Soulkey meet in the ASL Season 22 final

Rush, a Terran reaching his first ASL final in eight seasons, faces Soulkey, a veteran chasing the record for most ASL titles. The final is at the Lotte World ice rink in Seoul on Saturday 17 October at 3 p.m., as part of the Google Play ASL Season 22.

Source ↗

2026-10-16

ASL Season 22 final: Soulkey vs Rush on Saturday

The Google Play ASL Season 22 final is on Saturday, 17 October, at 15:00 at Lotte World Ice Rink in Seoul. Soulkey, a five-time champion, aims to keep Zerg on top, while Rush returns to the final hoping for his first ASL trophy after two runner-up finishes.

2026-10-16

Google Play showdown: Soulkey vs SnOw on Monday night

Soulkey and SnOw meet in a Google Play nine-set showdown on Monday, 19 October at 21:00 Korean time, and both play all nine sets even if one leads 8-0. Each set pays 100,000 won, doubled to 200,000 won with a daily double chance, and a whitewash adds 300,000 won, for a maximum of 1.4 million won. Each player removes one of the seven ASL Season 22 maps.
