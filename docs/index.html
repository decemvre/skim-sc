Mabeom Cup 5 and 6 tier: final 25 players set, wildcard match on Sunday

The final list for the Mabeom Cup 5 and 6 tier tournament was posted on 22 October, with 25 players. The top three Mabeom seeds and the top five by recommendations get round-of-16 seeds, and the next 15 enter the round of 24. The 24th and 25th places play a single wildcard match first, on Sunday 25 October at 3 pm, then the bracket draw and map pinball are held on site. Mabeom's Zerg group also had a group glamping trip on 22 October.
